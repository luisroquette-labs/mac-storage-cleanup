§ 01

How the recovery works

Entry 01 — Inventory

Scan the exact targets

List what would be touched and estimate the impact before you choose anything. No generic sweeps.

Entry 02 — Guard

Block what's running

Active processes and protected directories stay untouched, so sessions, credentials, and project state survive.

Entry 03 — Verify

Stage, then confirm

Move into recoverable staging first, verify hashes and manifests, then close the case with a written report.
